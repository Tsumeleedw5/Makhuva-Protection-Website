Our Story

Quality Is Our
Strategy.

Since 2013, Makhuva Protection Services has grown without advertising. Every client arrives via referral — the only metric that truly matters in this industry.

"Built on
Referrals.
Proven by
Results."

Makhuva Protection Services was founded in Germiston, Gauteng, in 2013 by individuals with deep roots in the South African private security sector. The founding principle was simple: build a company so reliable that marketing becomes unnecessary. In over a decade of operation, that principle has held.

We have never run a billboard campaign. We have never cold-called a prospect. Every client we serve today came to us because someone we already protected — a business owner, a property manager, a municipal official — told them we were the right company. That is the only endorsement we need.

Today Makhuva Trading Pty. Ltd. operates across multiple sectors in Gauteng, employing trained, vetted security professionals and support staff who share our commitment to doing the job properly — every shift, without exception.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

01
Reliability

A security provider that sometimes shows up is not a security provider. We hold every shift, every post, every commitment — without exception. Our clients plan around us because we have never given them a reason not to.

02
Integrity

Our personnel are background-checked, our operations are transparent, and our billing is straightforward. We do not over-promise. We do not cut corners. We do not place untrained people in positions that demand trained professionals.

03
Discretion

Security work requires confidentiality. Details of our clients' sites, arrangements, and vulnerabilities are never shared. Our officers understand that professionalism includes knowing what not to say — and to whom.
