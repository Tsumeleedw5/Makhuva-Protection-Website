Who We Protect

Sectors We Serve.

Six distinct sectors. One uncompromising standard. Makhuva adapts its approach to each environment — our quality of service never changes.

Government

Government facilities carry unique security demands — visible deterrence, strict access control, and personnel trained in handling sensitive environments with the professionalism and compliance awareness this level requires. Makhuva provides guarding and monitoring services to government departments and agencies across Gauteng.

  • Access control and visitor management systems
  • Armed and unarmed uniformed guarding
  • Offsite monitoring and alarm response
  • Government-format incident reporting
Municipalities

Municipal assets — depots, offices, water infrastructure, parks, and public spaces — face ongoing exposure to theft, vandalism, and unauthorised access. Our municipal security programmes protect physical infrastructure while maintaining the professional, public-facing conduct this environment demands.

  • Infrastructure and depot guarding
  • Mobile patrol of municipal assets
  • Public space security presence
  • Rapid response to theft and vandalism
Residential

Gated estates, townhouse complexes, and sectional title developments require security that residents actually trust — not just a uniform at a gate. We provide access management, perimeter monitoring, armed response, and community-patrol services that integrate seamlessly with your body corporate or homeowners' association.

  • Gate access control and visitor logging
  • 24/7 armed response coverage
  • Perimeter patrol and CCTV monitoring
  • Body corporate and HOA reporting
Commercial

Retail centres, office parks, warehouses, and logistics facilities operate with a mix of staff, visitors, and valuable stock. We design security programmes that protect your assets without interrupting legitimate operations — balancing deterrence with a professional, customer-appropriate presence on the floor.

  • Retail loss prevention and floor presence
  • Office park access and visitor management
  • Warehouse and logistics perimeter security
  • CCTV and alarm system integration
Industrial

Industrial sites — manufacturing plants, processing facilities, and power infrastructure — operate with high-value equipment, hazardous materials, and round-the-clock activity. Our industrial security teams are trained for the environment, equipped appropriately, and briefed on your specific site protocols before first deployment.

  • 24-hour site guarding and mobile patrol
  • Vehicle and contractor access management
  • CCTV coverage of high-value equipment areas
  • Health and safety compliant operations
Schools & Education

Schools and educational campuses require security that protects without intimidating. Our personnel deployed to educational environments are specifically selected for their communication skills and maturity — capable of managing student interactions, parent access, and after-hours protection with equal professionalism.

  • Morning and afternoon access management
  • Perimeter security and CCTV monitoring
  • After-hours and weekend guarding
  • Visitor logging and trespass prevention

"Makhuva Protection Services is trusted across Gauteng by organisations that cannot afford to compromise on security."

From gated communities to government facilities — we deliver the same uncompromising standard, regardless of sector or scale.
