Makhuva Protection Services was founded in Germiston, Gauteng, in 2013 on a single principle: build a company so reliable that marketing becomes unnecessary. In over a decade of operation, that principle has held.

We have never run a billboard campaign. Every client we serve today came to us because someone we already protected told them we were the right company. That is the only endorsement we need.

We operate across government, municipalities, residential estates, commercial sites, industrial facilities, and educational institutions — deploying trained, vetted personnel who share our commitment to doing the job properly, every shift, without exception.
