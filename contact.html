Common Questions

Before You Enquire

Do you operate outside of Germiston?

Yes. While our base of operations is in Germiston, we service clients across Gauteng. Contact us to confirm coverage for your specific location.

Are your guards PSIRA registered?

Yes. All security personnel deployed by Makhuva Protection Services are registered with the Private Security Industry Regulatory Authority (PSIRA) as required by South African law.

How quickly can you deploy?

Deployment timelines depend on scope, staffing requirements, and site briefings. In most cases we can deploy within 48–72 hours of a signed agreement for standard guarding requirements.

Do you offer combined services?

Yes. Many of our clients use a combination of physical guarding, armed response, and CCTV monitoring under a single service agreement. Bundled services are often the most cost-effective solution.

Is there a minimum contract term?

Contract terms are negotiated on a per-client basis and depend on the type of service. We are transparent about terms upfront — no hidden lock-in clauses.

How do I report a concern about service?

All clients have direct access to a Makhuva management contact. Concerns are acknowledged same-day and investigated within 24 hours. We take accountability seriously.
