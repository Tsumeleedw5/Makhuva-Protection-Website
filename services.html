What We Do

Our Services.

From uniformed guarding to electronic surveillance — the full spectrum of physical and technical security, built for Gauteng's most demanding environments.

01
Physical Guarding

Our uniformed guards are the most visible line of your defence. Each officer is PSIRA-registered, vetted through comprehensive background checks, and trained to Makhuva's internal standards — which exceed the statutory minimum. We deploy on fixed post, mobile patrol, and hybrid schedules based on your specific risk profile. Shift coverage is 24 hours, 7 days a week, including public holidays.

  • PSIRA-registered personnel on every deployment
  • 24/7 shift coverage including public holidays
  • Fixed post, mobile patrol, and hybrid options
  • Regular supervisory visits and incident reporting
  • Uniformed and plainclothes configurations available
02
Armed Response

When seconds matter, our armed response units are already on their way. Makhuva operates rapid-deployment armed vehicles across our service area in Gauteng. Response officers are trained in threat assessment, de-escalation, and use of force — and are equipped for the full range of scenarios from false alarm verification to active-threat management.

  • Rapid-response vehicles across Gauteng
  • Threat assessment and de-escalation trained
  • Panic button and duress code integration
  • Coordination with law enforcement where required
  • Client communication throughout every incident
03
Offsite Monitoring

Our dedicated control room provides continuous remote monitoring of CCTV feeds, alarm signals, and access control events — around the clock. Trained operators follow strict escalation procedures and maintain incident logs for every event. Available as a standalone service or fully integrated with our armed response and on-site guarding solutions.

  • 24/7 control room staffed by trained operators
  • CCTV, alarm, and access control monitoring
  • Real-time escalation to armed response or management
  • Detailed incident logs and monthly reporting
  • Compatible with most major alarm and camera brands
04
Investigations

Some threats are not visible at the gate. Our investigative division provides discreet, professional fact-finding for corporate, civil, and criminal matters. We conduct workplace theft investigations, pre-employment verification, insurance fraud enquiries, surveillance operations, and general due diligence — all within the boundaries of South African law.

  • Workplace theft and fraud investigations
  • Pre-employment and supplier background checks
  • Covert surveillance (legally compliant)
  • Insurance fraud and asset recovery support
  • Court-ready documentation of findings
05
Electronic Security

Physical security is only as strong as the technology behind it. Makhuva designs, supplies, installs, and maintains complete electronic security systems — including CCTV camera networks, electric fencing, access control (biometric and card-based), alarm systems, and intercom infrastructure. We work with commercial, residential, and industrial sites of all scales.

  • CCTV design, installation, and maintenance
  • Electric fence installation and monitoring
  • Biometric and card-based access control
  • Alarm system installation and management
  • Intercom and gate automation systems
06
VIP Escorting

High-profile individuals and high-value cargo require a different class of protection. Our close protection officers provide professional escort services — whether for a single transit, an extended assignment, or an ongoing engagement. We plan routes, assess venues, and coordinate logistics to ensure your principal or assets arrive safely every time.

  • Trained close protection officers (CPOs)
  • Route planning and advance venue assessment
  • High-value asset and cash-in-transit escort
  • Dignitary and executive protection
  • Discreet plainclothes or uniformed options
07
Cleaning Services

A secure environment is also a clean one. Makhuva's cleaning division delivers professional commercial and industrial cleaning services to the same standard as our security operations — rigorous, reliable, and consistent. We supply fully equipped cleaning teams for offices, industrial facilities, shopping centres, and institutional buildings.

  • Daily, weekly, and contract cleaning schedules
  • Industrial and commercial-grade equipment
  • Fully vetted and uniformed cleaning staff
  • Waste management and hygiene supply
  • Responsive to ad-hoc deep-clean requests
08
Gardening Services

The exterior of your premises reflects the professionalism of what happens inside. Our grounds maintenance teams provide routine landscaping, lawn care, irrigation management, and general site upkeep for commercial campuses, residential estates, government properties, and institutional clients. Trusted, insured, and contract-based.

  • Lawn mowing, edging, and routine maintenance
  • Tree trimming and shrub management
  • Irrigation system maintenance and repair
  • Seasonal planting and landscape design
  • Site clear-ups and refuse removal
